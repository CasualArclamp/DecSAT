panes: spectrum · waterfall (newest at the top) · constellation · power against time, all live

Every pane is drawn from a model of the signal, not a recording: the shapes are the ones the waterfall, the Identify constellation and the burst analyser show on the real carriers, with the numbers DecSAT measured on Horizons 3e and Intelsat 19. The panes run live, with signal time slowed so that a pane's window passes in about six seconds; the Es/N0 control sets the noise everywhere, and Pause freezes the lot. The one thing a pane cannot show is the bit-level fingerprint a decoder sees, so each card says what that is.
